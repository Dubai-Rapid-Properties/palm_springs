Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how D|R|P (Dubai Rapid Properties), a real estate brokerage based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, collects and uses personal information when you register your interest through our website, landing pages or advertising forms (including Facebook and Instagram lead forms).

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide: name, phone / WhatsApp number and email address.
  • Your property preferences: unit type, budget, purpose of purchase and timeline.
  • Basic campaign information, such as which advertisement or link brought you to us.

How we use it

  • To contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email about the project you enquired about.
  • To send you brochures, floor plans, prices, payment plans and launch updates.
  • To tell you about similar properties that may suit you, if you agreed to this.
  • To measure and improve our marketing.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with the relevant property developer only to register your interest or process a booking you ask for, and with service providers who help us operate (for example messaging, email or CRM tools). We may also disclose it where the law requires.

Retention

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law, and then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you. Just reply "STOP" to any message or email us at the address below.
