FAQ

Good questions

Who is the developer of Palm Springs?

Palm Springs is an upcoming community by Nakheel, the master developer behind Palm Jumeirah and Palm Jebel Ali.

Where exactly is it located?

Right below Palm Jebel Ali, on Sheikh Zayed Road in Jebel Ali. It is close to Jebel Ali Port, JAFZA and Expo City, and about 15 minutes from Al Maktoum International Airport (DWC).

What unit types are available?

3 & 4 bedroom townhouses and 4 & 5 bedroom standalone villas. Final layouts and sizes will be confirmed at launch.

What are the expected prices?

Prices are expected to start from around AED 3.8M and go up to around AED 9M, depending on unit type and size. Official pricing will be confirmed at launch.

When is the launch, and what's the payment plan?

The project has not been launched to the public yet. Register your interest and we'll share the launch date, payment plan and EOI process as soon as they're released.
