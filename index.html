The Opportunity

A Nakheel villa community at an accessible entry price

Palm Springs is Nakheel's next residential launch in Jebel Ali: a master-planned community of townhouses, standalone villas and lagoon-facing villas, set just below Palm Jebel Ali.

Townhouses are expected to start from AED 3.8M (≈ €925k), an unusually accessible entry point for a Nakheel villa address. The community sits in Dubai's southern growth corridor, close to Jebel Ali Port, JAFZA, Expo City and the new Al Maktoum International Airport.

The project has not been released to the public yet. Registering with D|R|P puts you first in line for the brochure, floor plans, price list and payment plan.

    FAQ

    Your launch questions, answered

    Who is the developer of Palm Springs?

    Palm Springs is an upcoming community by Nakheel, a member of Dubai Holding and the master developer behind Palm Jumeirah and Palm Jebel Ali.

    What is the starting price?

    Starting from AED 3.8M* (≈ €925k) for a 3-bedroom mid-unit townhouse, up to AED 8.9M (≈ €2.17M) for a 5-bedroom villa. 4-bedroom townhouses are AED 4.6M–5.2M and the 4-bedroom villa is AED 7.0M. *Indicative, subject to Nakheel's confirmation.

    What unit types and sizes are available?

    3BR mid-unit townhouse (G+1, ~2,329 sq ft), 4BR corner townhouse Type 1 (G+1, ~2,672 sq ft), 4BR corner townhouse Type 2 (G+2, ~3,115 sq ft), 4BR villa (G+2, ~4,041 sq ft) and 5BR villa (G+2, ~4,921 sq ft), plus a collection of lagoon villas. Final layouts are confirmed at launch.

    What is the payment plan?

    The expected plan is 60% during construction and 40% on handover: 10% on booking, then 10% in Apr 2027, Sep 2027, Feb 2028 and Jul 2028, 5% in Dec 2028 and May 2029, and 40% on handover (expected Oct 2030). The 4% DLD fee is paid separately on booking. Subject to Nakheel's confirmation.

    Does buying here qualify for a UAE Golden Visa?

    Property purchases of AED 2M and above can qualify for the UAE 10-year Golden Visa, subject to the criteria in force when you apply. Our team can walk you through the process.

    Where exactly is it located?

    In Jebel Ali, just below Palm Jebel Ali and close to Sheikh Zayed Road, Jebel Ali Port, JAFZA, Expo City Dubai and Al Maktoum International Airport (DWC). See the interactive map above for drive times.
