A garden neighbourhood, moments from the sea

Palm Springs is Nakheel's next residential community in Jebel Ali: Aliso townhouses and Rancho villas arranged around lagoons, water gardens and shaded walks, with Palm Jebel Ali just to the north.

It is an unusually accessible way into a Nakheel villa address, set in Dubai's southern growth corridor between Jebel Ali Port, Expo City and the new Al Maktoum International Airport.

    Who is the developer of Palm Springs?

    Palm Springs is an upcoming community by Nakheel, a member of Dubai Holding and the master developer behind Palm Jumeirah and Palm Jebel Ali.

    What is the starting price?

    Starting from AED 3.8M* (≈ €925k) for a 3-bedroom Aliso townhouse, up to AED 8.9M (≈ €2.17M) for a 5-bedroom Rancho villa. 4-bedroom Aliso townhouses are AED 4.6M–5.2M and the 4-bedroom Rancho villa is AED 7.0M. *Indicative, subject to Nakheel's confirmation.

    What unit types and sizes are available?

    Aliso townhouses: 3-bedroom middle unit (G+1, ~2,329 sq ft covered on a ~2,178 sq ft plot), 4-bedroom corner Type 1 (G+1, ~2,672 sq ft on ~3,269 sq ft) and 4-bedroom corner Type 2 (G+2, ~3,115 sq ft on ~3,435 sq ft). Rancho villas: 4-bedroom (G+2, ~4,041 sq ft on ~5,158 sq ft) and 5-bedroom (G+2, ~4,921 sq ft on ~6,065 sq ft). Final layouts are confirmed at launch.

    What is the payment plan?

    The expected plan is 60% during construction and 40% on handover: 10% on booking, then 10% in Apr 2027, Sep 2027, Feb 2028 and Jul 2028, 5% in Dec 2028 and May 2029, and 40% on handover (expected Oct 2030). The 4% DLD fee is paid separately on booking. Subject to Nakheel's confirmation.

    Does buying here qualify for a UAE Golden Visa?

    Property purchases of AED 2M and above can qualify for the UAE 10-year Golden Visa, subject to the criteria in force when you apply. Our team can walk you through the process.

    Where exactly is it located?

    In Jebel Ali, just below Palm Jebel Ali and close to Sheikh Zayed Road, Jebel Ali Port, JAFZA, Expo City Dubai and Al Maktoum International Airport (DWC). See the interactive map above for drive times.
